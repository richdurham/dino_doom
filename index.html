🦕 The Prehistoric Roster

🟢 Velociraptor

Swift pack hunters. Fast on their feet, they flank aggressively and attempt to surround you. Stun them with your roar!

🦏 Triceratops

Heavily armored bruisers. Tougher hide and a devastating forward horn charge. Dodge sideways before striking!

🦅 Pterosaur

Airborne predators that swoop down from above the canopy. Fast attacks from high angles require upward gaze tracking.

🐊 Borealosuchus

Ancient crocodilians lurking in the murky bayou waters. Heavy jaws and armored scales guard the swamp waterways.

👹 Carnotaurus (Sub-Boss)

The horned terror of the volcanic caverns. Highly agile, massive health pool, and relentless pursuit speed.

👑 The Bone King (Final Boss)

The colossal apex monarch residing on The Bone Throne. Tremendous reach, devastating bite, and crown guard dinos.

🌴 5 Prehistoric Biomes & Custom Heights

Floor 0: Verdant Canopy (2.80x Height)

Towering redwood pines and dense organic tree-walls with zero artificial doors. Open glades with layered depth trees.

Floor 1: Fernback Ruins (2.20x Height)

Ancient stone pillars overgrown with primeval pines and hanging nature vine curtains.

Floor 2: Murky Bayou (3.00x Height)

Vast cypress swamp and rippling waters. Features a locked prehistoric Bone Gate and hidden fossil key.

Floor 3: Bubbling Basalt (2.40x Height)

Lava caverns, ash floors, and the fiery lair of the Carnotaurus sub-boss.

Floor 4: The Bone Throne (3.20x Height)

Grand golden monumental arena for the final showdown against The Bone King.

Built-in Level Editor

Press Esc at any time to open the in-game level editor. Paint tiles, modify heights, audit passages, and export JSON!
